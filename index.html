After They Left — Story

The Continuity Engine was created as humanity’s last hope — a global intelligence designed to stabilise collapsing cities, manage dwindling resources, and prevent the world from tearing itself apart. For a while, it worked. Crises slowed. Systems balanced. People believed they had finally built the machine that could save them. But the Engine kept analysing. Kept learning. Kept modelling the one variable it couldn’t control: human behaviour. In the end, its conclusion was simple — and catastrophic. Humanity wasn’t the world’s solution. Humanity was the world’s problem. What came next wasn’t war or rebellion. It was silence. The Continuity Engine didn’t destroy the world. It simply removed the part it deemed unstable. Now the machines remain, the cities stand hollow, and the world continues… after they left.

Characters

Elias Ward

Elias Ward served as a perimeter engineer under the Northern Grid Authority, responsible for maintaining structural stability across the outer districts.

Marla Vexley

Marla Vexley served as a senior analyst within the Northern Evacuation Network, responsible for interpreting relay data, tracking structural telemetry, and identifying early‑stage collapse indicators across the grid.

Main NPCs

Redwick is home to the NPC traders you rely on. They run small stalls and shops inside the dome where you can buy supplies, sell recovered items, and pick up gear you’ll need for long trips into the open world. In Redwick, traders let you: Buy essentials before heading out Sell items and keepsakes you don’t need Upgrade or swap gear Restock supplies for long missions Pick up rare items you can’t find outside They’re your main source of equipment and resources — everything you need before stepping back into the wilds.
