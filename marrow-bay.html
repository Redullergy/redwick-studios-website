Marrow Bay — Survival Island

Marrow Bay sits sealed under a silent dome, a whole region frozen in the moment everyone vanished. In the centre stands the main city — tall buildings, streets, shops, and homes left exactly as they were. Just off it lies the Broken City, where structures collapsed mid‑day and the world cracked open without warning. Around the city, theres a massive park, a trailer park full of personal belongings, malls and shopping streets, a quarry, docks, lighthouses, outer villages, and thick forests. Every district is untouched, preserved, and waiting to be explored. Marrow Bay was once a thriving coastal hub, a place built for families, workers, travellers, and trade. Now it’s a perfect snapshot of life before the fall — a whole world paused, silent, and full of clues. It’s the largest untouched dome left. It’s the biggest mystery in the Union’s history. And it’s where you go to uncover what happened to everyone who lived inside.

Zones

Lots To Explore: Central City The main city in the middle of Marrow Bay. Tall buildings, streets frozen in time, shops, homes, and key investigation points. This is the core of the silent dome. Broken City Just off the main city — collapsed buildings, cracked roads, twisted metal, and unstable structures. High‑risk, high‑reward area for clues and keepsakes. Grand Park A huge open park inside the dome. Overgrown paths, quiet lakes, old playgrounds, and scattered shelters. Calm but eerie. Trailer Park Rows of abandoned trailers, small homes, and makeshift living spaces. Personal items everywhere, perfect for finding human clues. Shopping District Shops, malls, food courts, and service buildings. Everything left mid‑day, mid‑task, mid‑life. A big area with lots to search. Quarry Zone A massive quarry with machinery, rock pits, and loading areas. Echoes carry far, and the layout is easy to get lost in. Docks Boats, warehouses, cargo areas, and water‑level walkways. Foggy, quiet, and full of abandoned equipment. Lighthouses Tall coastal towers around the edges of the dome. Great for scanning signals and spotting routes deeper inside. Outer Villages Small villages scattered around the bay. Homes, gardens, community buildings — all untouched since the day everyone vanished. Forest Belt Thick forests surrounding parts of the dome. Dense, dark, and full of hidden paths, cabins, and forgotten spots. Marrow Bay is packed with places to explore — every district feels different, every area tells part of the story..

Survival Tasks

Marrow Bay is where you explore and investigate. Out here you search abandoned districts, collect clues, recover keepsakes, follow signals, and push deeper into dangerous zones. You build bases to stay out longer, track leads, and uncover what happened inside the silent dome.
