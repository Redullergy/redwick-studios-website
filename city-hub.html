Redwick — City Hub

Redwick sits on a coastal island surrounded by water, sealed beneath a humming EMP dome. The city is frozen in a 1950s aesthetic — neon signs, brick towers, diners, old cars — but threaded with modern tech that keeps it alive. It’s a place where the past and future collide, preserved and advanced at the same time. Before the fall, Redwick was the heart of the Continental Union. Its ports, factories, and streets powered the nation’s growth. Its people shaped its culture. Its skyline defined its identity. Now the dome keeps Redwick standing while the world outside shifts and decays. It’s the last active city, the final echo of the Union’s golden age, and the only place where life still moves beneath the crackling sky. Redwick is history preserved. Redwick is the future struggling to survive. Redwick is what remains of the Continental Union’s beating heart.

Districts

Main Market: The centre of Redwick. Busy stalls, traders, food stands, supply shops, and repair booths. It’s where everyone goes first — loud, bright, and always moving.
Garage District : A stripped‑back industrial zone filled with workshops, tool sheds, and vehicle bays. This is where gear gets fixed, upgraded, or rebuilt. The hum of machinery never stops.
Park District (Across the Bridge) : Cross the old bridge and you reach the quiet side of Redwick — a large park surrounded by 1950s‑style buildings and modern tech woven through them. It’s the calmest part of the dome, a place people go to breathe.

Activities

Redwick gives you things to do between missions. You can take on contracts, talk to NPCs, visit the shooting range to test your weaponry, grab food, rest, and sleep. It’s the place where you prepare, recover, and get ready for your next trip into the open world.
